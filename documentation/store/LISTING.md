## English

**Short description (max 80)**
`Scan letters, get key facts and chat with them. All offline on your phone.` (73)

**Full description (max 4000)**

```
Scan your paper mail and let an AI that lives on your phone read it for you. No account. No cloud. Your letters never leave your device.

WHAT IT DOES
- Scan letters with the camera or import images, with automatic edge detection and cleanup.
- AI extraction: sender, recipient, dates, deadlines, reference numbers and amounts are pulled out of each letter for you.
- A short summary, so you see what a letter is about before reading it.
- Confirm, Edit or Ignore every extracted value. You decide what is kept.
- Chat with your documents: ask "How much do I owe and by when?" and get an answer with sources. Tap a source to see the page with the passage highlighted.
- Profiles for you, your family or your household, with saved details such as addresses, so letters are matched to the right person.
- Trash with restore, so a wrong tap is never final.
- PDF export and sharing when you choose.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
- Fully offline after setup: text recognition and AI run on your phone.
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.
- Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern.
- The internet is used only to download the AI model, once. Your documents are never uploaded.

LANGUAGES
Letters in German and English are recognised and understood. The app is available in German, English and Arabic. Arabic letters: text recognition for Arabic is not yet supported.

HONEST LIMITS
- The first start needs an internet connection to download an AI model (from about 0.5 GB up to a few GB depending on the model you pick). After that, everything works offline.
- The AI runs on your phone, so it is a small model. It can make mistakes or miss details. Always check important values - amounts, dates, reference numbers - against the original letter before you act.
- Arabic text recognition is not yet supported in this version; German and English letters work best.
- Speed depends on your phone; newer devices with more memory are faster.
- Not a replacement for legal, tax or financial advice.

Questions? alyabroudy1@gmail.com
```
